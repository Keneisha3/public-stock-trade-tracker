# Canadian Depositary Receipts (CDRs) for the copycat's picks

1 of 52 US companies have a CDR that trades in Canadian dollars on Cboe Canada. A CDR is currency-hedged: it follows the US share in US dollars, minus a small hedging cost, so it differs from holding the US shares whenever the US dollar moves against the Canadian dollar.
